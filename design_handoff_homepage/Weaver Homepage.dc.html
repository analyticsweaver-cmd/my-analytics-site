How it works
Rate, project, then grade
01
Rate every team
A Power Score built from personnel, coaching, transfer portal movement, and returning production.
02
Project the season
Ratings become matchup margins and win probabilities. The NFL model simulates the season 20,000 times for win totals and playoff odds.
03
Grade the guesses
Every projection is checked against 2023–2025. Features that don't improve accuracy come out — scheme continuity did, and the win correlation rose from 0.352 to 0.382.
