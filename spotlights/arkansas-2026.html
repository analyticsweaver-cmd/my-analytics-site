How to read the grid below

Every position group shows three columns pulled straight from CFBD's roster, transfer portal, snap-usage, draft, and recruiting endpoints: who departed the 2025 roster, who's returning, and who's incoming for 2026. The bar under each name is that player's snap-share (or, for incoming high school signees with no college snaps yet, their recruiting rating instead — labeled as such) — so a departed star reads differently than a departed backup.

Where this is a guess, not a fact: CFBD doesn't publish next season's roster until closer to fall camp, so "returning" here is reconstructed — last year's roster, minus anyone found in the transfer portal or NFL draft data, minus year-4+ players not accounted for elsewhere (labeled "presumed departed," since a redshirt senior using a 5th year would incorrectly land in that bucket). The transfer-portal match to a player's prior-team snap share is a name match, not a shared ID — a small number of transfers (suffixes, nicknames) fall back to recruiting rating only. Treat single-player details as directionally right, not certified.
